## Trademark Considerations

Before finalizing any name:
1. ✅ Check USPTO trademark database
2. ✅ Google the name + your industry
3. ✅ Check social media (@username availability)
4. ✅ Verify no negative associations
5. ✅ Test internationally (doesn't mean bad words elsewhere)

The name should:
